Terms & conditions

Refer & Earn

01 · Referral commission

Earn 5% on eligible funds added by a friend who joins through your referral link. An eligible ₹500 top-up gives you ₹25.

02 · Claiming rewards

When a reward is ready, open Rewards and tap Claim beside that referral. The amount moves into your available balance. A reward can only be claimed once.

03 · Minimum payout

Your available balance must reach ₹100 before you can request a payout. Individual rewards can be claimed before you reach this amount.

04 · Pending rewards

Joining alone does not earn a commission. A reward remains pending until an eligible top-up has been confirmed.

05 · Eligible referrals

Your friend must create a new account using your invite. The invite must be recorded within 24 hours of signup and before their first payment or order. An account can have only one referrer. You cannot refer yourself. Free credits and promotional bonuses do not earn commission.
