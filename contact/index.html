Support

Contact Us

We're committed to providing reliable support and assistance to all our users. If you have any questions, concerns, or require help regarding your account, orders, payments, or our services, we're here to help.

01

Customer Support

You may contact us for assistance related to:

  • Account issues
  • Wallet and payment inquiries
  • Order status
  • Service-related questions
  • Refund requests
  • Cancellation requests
  • Technical issues
  • General feedback or suggestions
02

Response Time

We aim to respond to all inquiries as quickly as possible.

Response times may vary depending on:

  • The number of support requests
  • Business hours
  • The complexity of the issue
  • Verification requirements

Some requests may require additional information before they can be resolved.

03

Before Contacting Us

To help us assist you faster, please include relevant information such as:

  • Your registered email address
  • Order ID (if applicable)
  • Payment amount
  • Transaction ID or UTR (for payment-related inquiries)
  • A clear description of the issue
  • Screenshots (if available)

Providing complete information helps reduce delays in resolving your request.

04

Business Hours

Our support team operates during our regular business hours.

Response times outside business hours may be longer.
